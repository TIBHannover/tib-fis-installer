# TIB VIVO Installer 
This is a customized installer for the [VIVO](https://vivoweb.org) at TIB: https://vivo.tib.eu/fis. It serves as an institutional research profile system and research information system.

# Are you interested in working with VIVO? 
Please make use of the vast resources that are available about VIVO:
- [in the Lyrasis Wiki](https://wiki.lyrasis.org/display/VIVO)
- in the Github Repositories of 
  - [VIVO](https://github.com/vivo-project/VIVO)
  - [Vitro](https://github.com/vivo-project/Vitro)
  - [the VIVO Ontology](https://github.com/vivo-ontologies/vivo-ontology)

You can easily reach out to the VIVO Community [by joining the VIVO Slack](https://bit.ly/vivo-slack).

# Lab Group Open Research Information
Interested in our work? Please [visit our homepage](https://projects.tib.eu/openresearchinformation/en/), where you will find more information on who we are and what we are doing. You can find us in the VIVO Slack, too.

It uses the [three tiered build approach](https://wiki.lyrasis.org/display/VIVO/Building+VIVO+in+3+tiers) documented by the VIVO project.
